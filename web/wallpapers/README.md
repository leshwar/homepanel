# HomePanel scenic artwork

These are original procedural illustrations created for HomePanel, not photographs
of real places and not downloaded third-party wallpapers.

| Artwork | Description |
|---|---|
| Alpine dusk | Layered slate mountains, snow caps and a warm moon |
| Cedar mist | Evergreen silhouettes through cool forest haze |
| Quiet coast | Muted blue water, pale sand and coastal headlands |
| Desert glow | Sculpted warm dunes beneath a low sun |
| Starlit ridge | A quiet star field and dark mountain silhouettes |

Authorship: HomePanel contributors, with GitHub Copilot assistance.
Source: `scripts/build_wallpapers.py`; reproduce with
`.venv/bin/python scripts/build_wallpapers.py` (existing Pillow dependency).
No external imagery, fonts, textures, accounts or generation service is used.

The generated WebP images and their thumbnails are dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
To the extent possible under law, the contributors waive copyright and related
rights in these image assets. They are provided without warranty.

The 1600x1200 images are composed for centered cover-cropping in portrait and
landscape; 360x270 thumbnails keep the picker small. These shipped assets are
read-only. User uploads remain separate and retain their existing IDs.
